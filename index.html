Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Sudoku ("the App") does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

The App does not require an account or sign-in. It contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. It does not request any device permissions.

Game progress, settings, and statistics are saved only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

The App works offline and does not need an internet connection.

Children: The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
